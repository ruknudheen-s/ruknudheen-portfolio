About

Structured thinking, applied to messy data

I hold a Bachelor of Computer Applications and I'm currently deepening my analytics practice through a Data Analytics with Generative AI programme at Techolas Technologies. My work so far centers on taking inconsistent, real-world data and turning it into something a decision-maker can actually use — cleaning it, modelling it, and visualizing it in Power BI and Excel.

Both of my featured projects reflect that pattern: an academic-performance study that turned a raw survey into a dashboard about AI usage and burnout risk, and a full-stack expense tracker where I designed the data model behind the reporting. I care about the step before the chart — getting the data right — as much as the chart itself.
